## What I need to learn, and why it matters

**1. How "mastery" gets decided today**
- Get them describing how they actually tell a student gets it and is ready to level up.
- *Why it matters:* our whole reason to exist is "there's no real mastery check." If they can already tell fine at 5:1, the core problem weakens and the app needs a different justification.

**2. Whether anyone already quizzes or checks (Kahoot, Forms, etc.) — Jed especially**
- *Why:* if teachers already improvise quizzes, *that's* our real competition — not "nothing." Changes how we position the whole thing.

**3. Eric's bar for "worth building" vs. "waste of effort"**
- *Why:* this is the spec in disguise. His success line decides what v1 must do and what's out of scope.

**4. Do games / points / leaderboard count as "a quiz app" to him?**
- *Why:* your engagement vision lives or dies here. If he calls it scope creep, we build assessment-first and hold the fun stuff for later.

**5. A real story of a student who slipped through or got bored**
- *Why:* one concrete anecdote beats any amount of agreement. It's the evidence the problem is actually real, in their words.

**6. (light, near the end) Does the scattered-repos / course-hopping thing actually annoy anyone?**
- *Why:* the honest test for the all-in-one idea. If it bugs them unprompted, you've got a real opening. If not, it's only a problem in your head.
